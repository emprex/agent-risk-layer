Service scope

AgentRiskLayer provides human-led AI agent security assessments using ARL controls, evidence collection, authorised testing, remediation guidance and exact retesting within an agreed scope. The service supports security and deployment decisions; it is not an accredited certification, insurance product, legal opinion or guarantee that a system is risk-free.

Authorised testing

Testing is performed only against systems, environments and actions that the customer owns or is explicitly authorised to have assessed. The scope, permitted actions and material limitations are agreed before testing begins.

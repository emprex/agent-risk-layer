Governance

Complaints, assessment challenges and appeals

Concerns are recorded, reviewed against evidence and handled independently of commercial pressure. Raising a concern does not change the technical standard applied to an assessment.

What is a complaint?

A complaint is an expression of dissatisfaction about our service, conduct, scheme operation, assessor behaviour, public claims or another matter that is not itself a request to overturn a formal conformity or certification decision.

What is an appeal?

An appeal is a request by an affected party for reconsideration of a formal conformity or certification decision. AgentRiskLayer does not currently issue accredited certification. Where no formal conformity or certification decision exists, a technical disagreement or disputed assessment conclusion is handled through the assessment review and escalation process rather than represented as an accredited certification appeal.

How to raise a concern

Email support@agentrisklayer.com. Please include the assessment or service concerned, the issue or decision being challenged, relevant evidence, any confidentiality request and any conflict of interest you believe we should consider.

Do not send passwords, API keys, production secrets or unnecessary personal data by email.

What happens next?

We record the matter, triage whether it is a complaint, assessment challenge, security incident, appeal or other request, preserve the relevant assessment version and evidence, and identify any conflict or independence requirement before assigning the review.

The review may examine assessment records, applicable requirements, test evidence, relevant communications and whether controlled retesting or reassessment is required. Facts and conclusions are recorded separately. The outcome and rationale are documented to the extent permitted by confidentiality and legal obligations.

Independence

A person who made a disputed certification decision must not decide its appeal. A complaint about an assessor must not be investigated solely by that assessor. Where sufficient independence cannot be achieved internally, an external competent person or function must be used.

Confidentiality and fair treatment

Personal, commercial and security-sensitive information is handled on a need-to-know basis. Submitting a complaint, assessment challenge or appeal must not itself lead to discriminatory treatment or alter technical judgement.

Timing

We acknowledge and progress cases as promptly as practicable. Formal target times are still being validated as part of the ARL-AASC operating framework; independence and evidence quality will not be sacrificed to meet an arbitrary deadline. Material delays should be communicated and recorded.

Current status

AgentRiskLayer currently provides human-led security assessment and decision support. AGENTRISKLAYER LTD is not currently UKAS-accredited and does not currently issue accredited certification. This public process supports accountable assessment governance; it does not represent an accredited certification appeal mechanism.

The problem

An AI agent can cause harm without “hacking” anything.

Risk often comes from ordinary authority: broad permissions, untrusted instructions, connected tools, weak approvals or no reliable way to stop the agent.

01

What can it access?

Customer data, files, email, databases, cloud services, code and credentials.

02

What can it change?

Records, messages, permissions, deployments, money and irreversible business actions.

03

What can influence it?

Users, documents, websites, retrieved content and hidden instructions inside trusted workflows.

04

Who stays in control?

Whether important actions pause for a person, can be stopped quickly and leave reliable evidence.

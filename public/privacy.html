Privacy notice

This fallback file is not served by the production application. The live `/privacy.html` route is generated from the configured legal operator, business address, jurisdiction and support contact. Production startup fails closed until those factual owner-controlled values are present.

Product data boundary

Account, assessment, purchase, subscription, security-event and audit records are processed to provide and secure the service. Stripe handles live card data and Resend handles transactional delivery. Hosted Guard evidence excludes raw prompts, model responses and tool arguments; local inspection and red-team evidence excludes source code, raw transcripts, credentials and matched secret values.

Controls

Account holders can export their data and request permanent deletion, subject to records that must be retained by law or payment providers. Assessments are private by default.

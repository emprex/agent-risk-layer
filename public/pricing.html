Before you pay

What does the assessment payment do?

It unlocks the full report/PDF and the evidence, controlled-testing, remediation and exact-retest workflow. Only work actually completed is reported as performed.

Who performs inspection and controlled testing?

The customer operates the privacy-safe local tools when applicable. Payment does not itself perform human review, inspection, testing or independent verification.

How do ongoing plans differ?

Developer, Team and Agency provide the same ongoing-protection outcome at different capacity and collaboration levels. Current limits above come directly from the server catalogue.

Is Enterprise a certification?

No. Enterprise is a scoped commercial proposal, not an accredited certification or a guarantee that a system is risk-free.

Before you pay

The boundary is part of the product.

What does the £99 assessment payment do?

It unlocks the full report/PDF and the evidence, controlled-testing, remediation and exact-retest workflow for one agent. Only work actually completed is reported as performed.

Does £99 buy a human security review?

No. Purchasing does not itself perform a human review, inspection or test. The product records customer-operated inspection, controlled testing and other evidence only when those steps are actually completed.

When should I buy an ongoing plan?

Choose Developer, Team or Agency only when you need recurring runtime protection, more projects, longer retention or collaborative operation. You do not need a subscription just to start one assessment.

Is Enterprise a certification?

No. Enterprise is a scoped commercial proposal, not an accredited certification or a guarantee that a system is risk-free.

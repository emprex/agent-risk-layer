Honest boundaries

Capabilities we do not claim.

Trust improves when missing assurance is visible rather than disguised as a feature.

No automatic cloud-account discovery

AWS, Azure, GCP and browser estates require future authorised connectors and customer credentials.

No behavioural model-weight backdoor guarantee

Artifact checks cover provenance, digest, format and structural safety—not specialist behavioural research.

No accredited certification

The proprietary assessment supports decisions but is not an independent certification or guarantee of security.

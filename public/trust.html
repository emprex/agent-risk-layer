Evidence chain

What can support readiness

Evidence stays distinguishable by source and authority.

01Declared controls

Customer statements remain declared until supported.

02Observed controls

Reviewable behaviour or system records.

03Findings

Authoritative ARL security findings.

04Attack evidence

Results from bounded authorised tests.

05Runtime evidence

Recorded control behaviour where applicable.

06Human approval

Explicit accountable approvals when required.

07Remediation

Implementation evidence for the changed system.

08Retest

Exact verification against the affected control.

09Deployment decision

The final accountable decision remains human.

Production-readiness requirement

Readiness must come from authoritative controls and evidence, not static website copy or a billing state.

Privacy notice

Last updated 22 July 2026

Information collected

AgentRiskLayer stores account email addresses, salted password hashes, assessment answers and results, purchase and subscription references, delivery logs and basic product events. Live card details are handled by Stripe and are not stored by AgentRiskLayer.

Purpose

Information is used to provide assessments, generate reports, deliver purchases, maintain account history, prevent abuse and understand product performance.

Processors

When configured, Stripe processes payments and invoices, and Resend delivers transactional email. Hosting providers may process application and database data.

Retention and deletion

Production operators should define a retention schedule and provide an account-deletion route before public launch. This starter includes the product workflow but does not substitute for legal review.

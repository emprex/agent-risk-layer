Service scope

AgentRiskLayer provides human-led AI agent security assessments within an agreed scope and authorised test boundary. The service supports security and deployment decisions; it is not an accredited certification, insurance product, legal opinion or guarantee that a system is risk-free.

Authorised testing

Testing is performed only against systems and actions the customer owns or is explicitly authorised to have assessed.

Commercial terms

Scope, price, payment method, timing and any milestones are agreed directly before paid work begins. This website has no browser checkout or subscription service.

Evidence boundary

An assessment is bounded by the agreed scope, available evidence and the exact system state assessed. Unknown or untested areas remain limitations.

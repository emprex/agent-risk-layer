Boundary

Guardian discovers. AgentRiskLayer assesses.

Guardian observations are not findings, severity decisions, readiness decisions or deployment verdicts.

AgentRiskLayer uses scope, evidence, authorised testing, remediation and exact retesting to support the final human decision.

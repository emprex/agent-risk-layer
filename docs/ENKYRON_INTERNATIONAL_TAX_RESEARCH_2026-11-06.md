## 1. UK baseline (HMRC)

1. **VAT registration:** at the date of research, compulsory registration is generally triggered when UK taxable turnover over the last rolling 12 months exceeds **£90,000**, or is expected to exceed £90,000 in the next 30 days. Voluntary registration is possible below the threshold. Importantly, HMRC's VATREG02550 states supplies with a **place of supply outside the UK are disregarded** in calculating UK taxable turnover. Do **not** apply this threshold automatically to all global revenue.
   Sources: https://www.gov.uk/register-for-vat ; https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/vat-registration-manual/vatreg02550
2. **Taxable UK services:** UK VAT standard rate **20%** for most taxable services, *if* the supplier is VAT-registered and VAT is chargeable. HMRC explicitly warns not to show VAT separately on an invoice before obtaining the VAT registration number. No status or number for AGENTRISKLAYER LTD has yet been verified in this project.
   Sources: https://www.gov.uk/vat-rates ; https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/vat-notice-7001-should-i-be-registered-for-vat/vat-notice-7001-should-i-be-registered-for-vat
3. **B2B services general rule:** place of supply = where the **customer belongs**, subject to special exceptions. When the customer is genuinely a business established outside the UK and the general rule applies, the supply is **outside the scope of UK VAT** — *not* a UK zero-rated sale. Keep evidence proving business status and location (e.g. an EU VAT ID or other commercial evidence). Local tax liability may still apply.
4. **B2C services general rule:** place of supply normally = supplier's place. However, HMRC Notice 741A section 12 provides an important **exception for specified professional/advisory/consultancy services supplied by UK suppliers to consumers outside the UK**. Whether a particular cybersecurity assessment qualifies requires review of its *actual character*. A customer outside the UK does not automatically imply the same answer for every type of service.
   Source: https://www.gov.uk/guidance/vat-place-of-supply-of-services-notice-741a
5. **Automated digital products** differ from expert-led services. Where an electronically supplied service is delivered essentially automatically with minimal human intervention, the B2C customer location rules may apply. A report individually commissioned and prepared by humans is not automatically an e-service simply because delivered by PDF/email. Packages with automated and human elements require classification.
   Source: https://www.gov.uk/guidance/the-vat-rules-if-you-supply-digital-services-to-private-consumers

## 3. Beyond UK VAT

- **Withholding tax:** overseas jurisdictions sometimes require the buyer to retain an amount and pay it to the local tax authority. Seek appropriate treaty relief documentation *before final price and issuance*. UK government business guidance: https://www.business.gov.uk/export-from-uk/learn/categories/prepare-sell-new-country/selling-services-overseas/understanding-withholding-tax-when-exporting-a-service/
- **Company disclosure:** UK limited company business letters, order forms and website should state registered number, registered office, registration jurisdiction and limited status. No company number/address should be fabricated by ENKYRON. https://www.gov.uk/running-a-limited-company/signs-stationery-and-promotional-material
- **Invoices vs drafts:** an actual invoice must include further particulars, unique identifier, parties, billing address, description, dates, price and VAT where relevant; an unsent draft PDF is **not an invoice**. https://www.gov.uk/invoicing-and-taking-payment-from-customers/invoices-what-they-must-include
- **Sanctions screening and cybersecurity export controls:** ability to sell globally is not blanket permission. Check the customer/beneficial owner on the up-to-date UK sanctions sources and consider strategic controls where technology, data or technical assistance is transferred. https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/uk-sanctions ; https://www.gov.uk/guidance/uk-strategic-export-controls
- **International personal data:** initiating a restricted UK GDPR transfer may require an adequacy basis, appropriate safeguards and potentially a transfer risk assessment. https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/international-transfers/a-brief-guide-to-international-transfers/
